Leadership

Behind every system we build are more than twenty years of solving problems where there is no margin for error.

Arois Monsalve

Founder & CEO View LinkedIn profile ↗

A mechanical engineer by training, Arois Monsalve has spent more than 20 years working where operations cannot stop: supervising maintenance at an oil refinery, supporting commercial aircraft engines, running technology for a public institution, teaching engineering at university and coordinating teams of more than 250 people on infrastructure that millions now move through every day, including Santiago's airport and metro.

Technology was the common thread all along. From managing IT systems to analyzing aircraft engine data, Arois kept looking for ways to let machines do the heavy lifting, and kept seeing the same thing: talented teams trapped in manual processes nobody had time to fix.

When artificial intelligence arrived, everything clicked. For the first time, a lifelong passion for technology and two decades of field experience pointed in the same direction. DYNX Labs was born from that conviction: systems that give every team back its most valuable resource, time. Not to build demos. To make things work.
