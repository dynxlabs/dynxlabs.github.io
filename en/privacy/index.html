Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026 · Controller: Dynamic Neuromorphic Labs SpA (DYNX Labs)

At DYNX Labs we take care of your personal data. This policy explains, clearly, what data we process, why, on what legal basis, and what rights you have, wherever you are. The specifics for your country or region are at the end, in Section 15 (Regional provisions).

1. Who we are

Data controller: Dynamic Neuromorphic Labs SpA, operating as DYNX Labs, a company incorporated and domiciled in Chile. For any privacy matter, write to privacy@dynxlabs.com.

We process your data as a controller when we decide the purposes for which it is used (for example, your inquiries on the site). When we process data on behalf of a client who has contracted our services, we act as a processor and follow their instructions; in that case, that client's privacy policy also applies.

2. Scope

This policy covers the dynxlabs.com website, its contact forms, and our commercial communications. The processing of data within our contracted products and services is also governed by the applicable contract and data protection addendum.

3. Legal framework

We process your data in accordance with the data protection legislation applicable to you based on your location. As a general baseline, we apply principles common to those laws —lawfulness, purpose limitation, proportionality, transparency, security, and accountability— and we respect the rights each law recognizes. The specific rules by region (Chile, European Union/EEA, United Kingdom, Switzerland, Brazil, United States/California, among others) are detailed in Section 15.

4. What data we collect

We collect only what is necessary:

  • Contact: name, email, phone or WhatsApp, company.
  • About your inquiry: service of interest, industry, country/region, and whatever you choose to tell us about your need.
  • Technical browsing data: where applicable, data such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited, depending on the site's tools.

We do not request sensitive data. Please do not include it in the forms, nor third-party data without their authorization.

5. How we use it and on what basis

  • Respond to your inquiry and follow up commercially — basis: your consent when submitting the form and/or pre-contractual measures.
  • Manage the relationship with clients and suppliers — basis: performance of the contract.
  • Comply with legal obligations (e.g. tax) — basis: legal obligation.
  • Send you commercial communications, only if you authorize it — basis: consent (you can unsubscribe anytime).
  • Improve and protect our site and services (security, fraud prevention, analytics) — basis: legitimate interest, balanced with your rights.

8. International transfers

We operate nationally and internationally. Our providers (GitHub, Cloudflare, and Google) are located in the United States, so some of your data may be processed there. When we transfer data outside your country or region, we apply the safeguards required by applicable law to protect your information (for example, standard contractual clauses or other recognized mechanisms).

9. Artificial intelligence

Some of our services use AI models for tasks such as classifying information, generating responses, or summaries. AI outputs support human decisions and do not replace them in matters with legal effects or significant impact on individuals. We do not use your personal data to train general-purpose models.

10. How long we keep your data

We keep your data only as long as necessary for the purpose that originated it and to comply with legal obligations; we then delete or anonymize it. As a reference: commercial inquiries are kept for the duration of their handling and follow-up; accounting information, for the applicable legal periods.

11. Your rights

Regardless of your country, you can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure (cancellation), and objection —and any others your legislation recognizes— by writing to privacy@dynxlabs.com. We will respond within the legal timeframes and, as a commitment, within a maximum of 10 business days. We may ask you for information to verify your identity. Depending on your region, you may have additional rights and an authority to lodge a complaint with: see Section 15.

12. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data (access control, encryption in transit, security logs). No system is infallible, but we work to reduce the risks.

13. Minors

Our services are not directed to minors and we do not request their data through our commercial channels.

14. Third-party links

Our site may link to external pages. We do not control their privacy practices; we recommend reviewing their policies before providing them data.

15. Regional provisions

This section supplements the above with the rules specific to each region. In case of conflict between the general body and this section, the regional provision that applies to you prevails. The rights in Section 11 apply in all regions; below we indicate the additional rights and the authority with which you may file a complaint in your country.

15.2 European Union, European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, and Switzerland

If you are in these regions, we process your data in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and equivalent local laws (UK GDPR, Swiss FADP). In particular:

  • Legal bases: consent, performance of contract, legal obligation, or legitimate interest, as applicable (Section 5).
  • Expanded rights: in addition to those in Section 11, you have the right to restriction of processing, to portability, and to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • International transfers: when we transfer data outside the EEA/UK/Switzerland, we use recognized mechanisms (e.g. standard contractual clauses).
  • Supervisory authority: you may lodge a complaint with the authority in your country.
  • EU / UK representative: we have not yet appointed a representative; we will do so when we regularly process data of EEA/UK residents (art. 27 GDPR).

15.3 United States (including California)

If you reside in the United States, you may have rights under your state's law. In California (CCPA/CPRA) you have the right to know what information we collect, to access and delete your information, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising your rights. We do not sell or share your personal information within the meaning of those laws. To exercise these rights, write to privacy@dynxlabs.com.

16. Changes and contact

We will post any changes on this same page, with the update date. For privacy inquiries: privacy@dynxlabs.com · DYNX Labs.
